# Customer Refund Policy

## 1. Overview
All customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of product purchase or order cancellation. 

## 2. Cancelled Orders with Payment Deducted
If your order was cancelled due to inventory mismatch or payment gateway timeout, but funds were deducted from your bank account or credit card:
- The system automatically triggers an automated refund request within 2 hours.
- Refund processing time: 3 to 5 business days for credit cards, and 24 to 48 hours for UPI / Direct bank transfers.
- No cancellation charges apply for system-cancelled orders.

## 3. Expedited Refunds
For Platinum VIP or Gold loyalty members, support agents can trigger an instant wallet refund or priority card reversal upon verifying transaction receipt.
