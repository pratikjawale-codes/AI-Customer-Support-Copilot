# Payment & Transaction FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: My money was debited, but my order status says "Unpaid" or "Cancelled". Why?
This occurs when the payment gateway experiences temporary bank network latency. The money is held in escrow by the bank gateway and will either automatically convert to a confirmed order or trigger a reverse refund within 24 hours.

### Q: Which payment methods are accepted?
We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and UPI transfers.

### Q: What should I do if a double charge appears on my bank statement?
Provide the transaction IDs to customer support. Duplicate charges are automatically flagged by our accounting auditor and reversed within 1-2 business days.
