# Delivery & Shipping Policy

## 1. Shipping Timelines
- **Standard Shipping**: 3 - 5 business days.
- **Express Delivery**: 1 - 2 business days.
- **Same-Day Delivery**: Available in select metropolitan areas for orders placed before 11:00 AM.

## 2. Tracking Your Order
Once an order ships, a live tracking link and carrier tracking ID (e.g. FedEx / DHL / UPS) is sent via SMS and email. Customers can check the current status by querying `Where is order #ID`.

## 3. Delayed Deliveries & Compensation
If delivery exceeds 3 days past the estimated delivery date without carrier notice, customer support agents are authorized to issue a 15% goodwill discount code or refund standard shipping fees.
