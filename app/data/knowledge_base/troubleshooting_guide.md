# Technical & Login Troubleshooting Guide

## 1. Login & Password Reset Issues
If a customer cannot log into their account:
- Verify if the account email exists in the CRM system.
- Trigger a password reset link to their registered email address.
- Clear browser cookies & cache, or try Incognito mode.

## 2. Subscription & Account Access
If subscription benefits are not reflecting:
- Check subscription status via account dashboard.
- Force refresh token by signing out and logging back in.
- For billing sync errors, contact technical support for manual sync.
