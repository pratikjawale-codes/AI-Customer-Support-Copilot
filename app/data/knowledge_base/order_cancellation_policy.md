# Order Cancellation Policy

## 1. Cancellation Windows
- Orders in "Processing" status can be cancelled within 2 hours of placement for an instant automated refund.
- Shipped or In-Transit items cannot be cancelled directly; customers must request a return upon delivery.

## 2. Automatic System Cancellations
If an item becomes out of stock after order placement, our system automatically cancels the order, notifies the customer, and initiates a priority refund.
